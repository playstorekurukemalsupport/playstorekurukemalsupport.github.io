English

This policy applies to the Android game 2048 Puzzle - Number Game, package com.alikip.puzzle_2048. The game includes Google AdMob advertising. The currently distributed version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK; its data processing is separate from game records stored on your device.

Your game records

Scores, board state, achievements, statistics, daily challenges, sound and theme settings are stored on your device. Our game code does not send these records to an alico Apps game server. There is no application account or cloud score service. This does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no data.

Advertising data and recipients

When advertising runs, Google and its advertising services can receive device or advertising identifiers, an IP address indicating an approximate area, interactions with the app and advertisements, and diagnostic information about performance. These support advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The current version initializes advertising when the app starts. A reward-ad choice is not a switch that disables all advertising data collection.

The game does not request GPS, microphone, camera or contacts access. Approximate location inferred from the advertising connection can still be processed without a GPS permission. No payment-card information is requested by the game.

Choices and security

You can manage or delete your Android advertising ID in Android privacy settings and review Google advertising choices. Available settings depend on your Android version and region. Changing an identifier does not stop every category of SDK processing. Google documents encrypted transport for its Mobile Ads SDK. This policy does not promise that all advertising data remains on your device.

Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until app data is removed or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android's device backup and restore settings. Uninstalling does not erase records already held by an advertising provider. Google controls retention and deletion of its advertising data under its own policies; we do not invent a separate retention period or claim that an alico Apps account-deletion endpoint exists. Contact us for questions or assistance with applicable access or deletion rights.

See Google's privacy policy, Google services in partner apps and Mobile Ads data disclosures. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; ordinary website access may be logged by the hosting provider as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. Material changes are reflected by the date above and, where required, in the app. For questions about use by a child or privacy preferences, contact the publisher at the address above; this policy does not claim an age or consent gate that the installed version does not provide.
