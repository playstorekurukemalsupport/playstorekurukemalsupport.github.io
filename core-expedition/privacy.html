Core Expedition — Privacy Policy

Developer: Ali Kip. Application: com.alicoapps.core_expedition. Updated 5 October 2026. Türkçe

Local gameplay data

Core Expedition is an offline action game. This version does not create accounts, show advertising, offer purchases or include advertising or analytics SDKs. The game does not transmit your gameplay data to the developer or share it with advertisers. No sign-in is required.

Campaign progress, stars, credits, weapon upgrades, challenge records, language and gameplay preferences are saved inside the application's private storage on your device. A local recovery copy helps restore a damaged save. These files are not a developer-operated cloud backup. The application disables Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup for its app data.

Reset and deletion

Settings → Reset progress clears the current save, recovery copy and temporary save files, then starts a new local save. Clearing application data in Android settings or uninstalling the game also removes its local app data. The developer has no remote copy of your progress to restore or delete. No account deletion process is needed because this version creates no accounts.

External links and support

Privacy and support links open your browser or email application. Visiting this website may provide network information such as your IP address to the website hosting provider, GitHub Pages. Sending support email gives the developer the address and information you choose to include. Such messages are used to respond to your request, retained while needed for support or legal obligations, and may be deleted on request. Please do not send passwords or private device information.

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, store or operating-system diagnostic data under their own policies. This policy describes the game’s own behavior. GitHub privacy · Google privacy
